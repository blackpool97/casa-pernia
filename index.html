Our Story

The Minds Behind
the Experience

Two chefs. One shared vision. An unwavering commitment to extraordinary dining.

Chef Photo

Luis Roberto Pernia

Co-Founder & Private Dining Host

I have always been the person bringing people together around food. What started as cooking for friends and family turned into something more — creating full dinner experiences where people can relax and actually enjoy their time together.

I focus on planning, coordination, and making sure every detail of the night runs smoothly, so you don't have to think about anything except your guests.

Chef Photo

Mateo Bourdais

Co-Founder & Culinary Lead

I've always enjoyed cooking for others and seeing how food brings people together. Over time, that turned into creating meals that feel a little more special — the kind you don't usually make on a regular night.

I focus on building menus that are simple, thoughtful, and tailored to each dinner. My goal is to make food that feels elevated but still comfortable, so guests can enjoy the experience without it feeling too formal or overcomplicated.

For me, it's not just about the food, it's about helping create a night people actually remember.
